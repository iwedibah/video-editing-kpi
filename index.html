📋 Complete KPI Reference Guide

This comprehensive list defines all performance indicators for the Video Editing Department.

🎯 Technical Quality KPIs

1. Clean Audio
Noise reduction applied, balanced levels, no distortion
2. Color Grading
Consistent, professional color correction throughout
3. Captions/Subtitles
Error-free, well-timed, visible, proper formatting
4. Audio Mixing
Perfect blend of voice, music, SFX (contextual per video type)
5. Resolution & Aspect Ratio
Correct for platform (9:16, 16:9, etc.)
6. Smooth Transitions
No jarring cuts, professional B-roll integration

🎨 Content & Creative KPIs

7. Hook Effectiveness
First 3-5 seconds grab attention immediately
8. B-Roll Alignment
Visual elements support narrative/message
9. Pacing & Flow
Maintains engagement throughout entire video
10. Storytelling Quality
Clear narrative arc: hook → value → CTA

✅ Brand & Compliance KPIs

11. CTA Placement
Clear, correct, well-integrated call-to-action
12. Brand Standards
Colors, fonts, logo usage compliance
13. Copyright Compliance
All audio/visual assets licensed or royalty-free
14. Duration Compliance
Nuggets: 60-90s | Long-form: 15-20min | Highlights: 120-140s | Trailers: 50-60s

⏱️ Timeliness & Process KPIs

15. On-Time Delivery
Nuggets/Shorts: 24-48hrs | Long-form: 72hrs (3 days) | Highlights: <24hrs
16. Revision Speed
Corrections implemented within 24 hours of feedback
17. QA Pass Rate
Target: 70% of videos pass quality check on first submission
18. File Organization
Proper naming conventions and folder structure

🎬 Tier-Specific KPIs

19. Tier 1 Quality
Clean nugget extraction, logical flow, proper labeling
20. Tier 2 Enhancement
Motion graphics, music selection, professional polish
21. Feedback Implementation
Accurately incorporates notes quickly and effectively

🤝 Collaboration & Growth KPIs

22. Team Communication
Timely updates, proactive problem-solving
23. Documentation
Project files properly archived and accessible
24. Continuous Learning
Stays updated with trends, proposes improvements
25. Cross-Tier Support
Helps other tiers when needed, mentors juniors
Performance Ratings:
• Excellent (9-10): Bonus eligible, potential tier advancement
• Good (7.5-8.9): Standard performance, targeted development
• Needs Improvement (<7.5): Training plan, mentorship required
